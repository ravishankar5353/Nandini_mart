
# 🛒 MNM MART

### Fresh Groceries. Easy Shopping. Delivered to Your Doorstep.

**MNM MART** is a modern grocery e-commerce and doorstep delivery web application designed to make everyday grocery shopping simple, convenient, and accessible online.

The platform allows customers to browse grocery products, search and filter items, manage their cart and wishlist, place orders, and track orders through a complete **mandatory doorstep delivery** workflow.

# 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of **MNM MART** is to provide customers with a convenient online grocery shopping experience.

Customers can:

- Browse grocery products
- Search for products
- Explore product categories
- View product details
- Add products to cart
- Manage wishlist
- Enter delivery address
- Place orders
- Track orders
- Receive products through mandatory doorstep delivery

The project also includes an **Admin Dashboard** for managing products, categories, orders, customers, inventory, and delivery operations.

# 🚚 Mandatory Doorstep Delivery

One of the main features of **MNM MART** is **mandatory doorstep delivery**.

Customers must provide a valid delivery address before placing an order.

There is:

- ❌ No store pickup
- ❌ No click-and-collect
- ✅ Only doorstep delivery

### Delivery Flow

```text
Order Placed
      ↓
Order Confirmed
      ↓
Order Packed
      ↓
Out for Delivery
      ↓
Delivered
````

This makes MNM MART specifically focused on an online grocery delivery system.
